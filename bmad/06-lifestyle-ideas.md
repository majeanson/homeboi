# 06 · Lifestyle Ideas — 50 more, to agglomerate a whole life

> A second brainstorm, deliberately **past** the axes `05` already mined (board,
> kitchen, list, kids, shopping). The question here is different: _if a family ran
> their whole life through one calm wall tablet, what parts of life still live in
> drawers, group chats, fridge magnets, and three other apps — and could quietly
> move in?_ These reach into the home itself, money & admin, health, pets,
> transport, memory, and the household-as-an-organism.
>
> Same legend as `05`: **[S]** small / **[M]** medium / **[L]** large ·

## North star

Babillard already owns **today**. The frontier is **the slow stuff** — the
quarterly, the seasonal, the once-a-year, the "where is it / when is it due / who
do I call." That stuff is exactly what falls through the cracks of a calendar app
and a group chat, and exactly what a _calm, always-on, shared_ surface is uniquely
good at: it doesn't nag, it just **knows**, and it shows the one thing that's near.
The unifying move: **make the home, the money, and the body first-class "members"**
of the household alongside the people — each with its own quiet cadence of upkeep.

---

## A · The home as a living thing (upkeep & the house itself)

1. **Home upkeep cadence** [M] — furnace filter, smoke-detector batteries, water
   filter, gutter clean, descale the kettle: recurring home chores with long
   intervals that a calendar forgets. Surfaces only as a calm "due soon" line, never
   a backlog of red. _(reuse: recurrence engine + `tasks`; a "home" pseudo-member so
   it lands in Le cercle's cadence, not a person's chore rotation.)_
2. **Appliance & warranty vault** [M] — model numbers, purchase dates, warranty
   expiry, the manual PDF, the filter size you can never remember. Glance: "fridge
   warranty ends in 2 months." _(reuse: R2 for manuals/photos; cercle-businesses for
   the repair shop that services it.)_
3. **"Where's the…" house map** [S] ✦ — water shutoff, breaker panel, the spare key,
   the good scissors. A calm reference list of _locations_ (not quantities — stays
   inside the calm/no-inventory line). Gold for a babysitter or a new partner.
4. **Bin / recycling night** [S] — "blue bin out tonight," with the alternating
   recycling/compost week baked into the recurrence. One of the most-forgotten
   weekly household facts. _(reuse: recurrence + the "Bientôt" lead-time chip.)_
5. **Seasonal home rituals** [S] — swap winter tires, open the pool, storm windows
   up, plant the garden, change the clocks. A gentle seasonal checklist that appears
   in its month and disappears when done. _(reuse: `todos` templates, like departure
   checklists.)_
6. **Home projects ("someday/honey-do")** [M] — bigger-than-a-chore projects
   (paint the deck, fix the fence) parked without guilt; promote one to "this month"
   when there's room. Distinct from the chore rotation. _(reuse: `todos`.)_
7. **Plant & garden watering** [S] — per-plant or per-bed watering cadence, with
   weather-aware skips ("it rained — skip today"). _(reuse: recurrence + `lib/weather`.)_

## B · Money & admin (calm — dates and documents, never shame-graphs)

8. **Bill due-date board** [M] ⚠ — _when_ recurring bills land (hydro, rent,
   daycare, insurance), not a budget tracker. A calm "due in 3 days" chip, optional
   rough amount, never a balance-of-shame graph. _(reuse: recurrence + reminder chip.)_
9. **Subscription watch** [S] ⚠ — the streaming/SaaS renewals you forget you pay
   for, each with a renewal date and a one-tap "remind me to cancel before." Pure
   calm: it's a date list, not a spend dashboard.
10. **Important-document expiry** [S] — passports, driver's licence, health card
    (RAMQ), permits — each with an expiry date and a quiet lead-up. The single most
    "I wish I'd known a month earlier" category. _(reuse: birthdays-as-derived-dates
    pattern — no event rows, just derived "expires soon.")_
11. **Document vault** [M] — photos of the insurance card, the lease, the
    vaccination record, the car registration, stored against the person/home/vehicle
    they belong to. _(reuse: R2 + cercle entities.)_
12. **Shared "who paid" ledger** [M] ⚠ — co-parents/roommates log who covered a
    shared cost; shows contributions like the **chore ledger** (faces, not a
    running debt balance). Honours calm by refusing the "you owe me" number.
13. **Tax-season shoebox** [S] — a tagged folder you drop receipt/document photos
    into all year (childcare receipts, medical, donations) so February isn't a
    scramble. _(reuse: R2 + a tag, like recipe collections.)_

## C · The body & wellbeing (household-scale, no metrics-of-shame)

14. **Family medical card** [S] ✦ — allergies, blood types, current meds, doctor &
    pharmacy numbers, RAMQ. One glance in an emergency; visible in **guest mode** for
    the babysitter. _(reuse: cercle member fields + businesses for the clinic.)_
15. **Medication & vitamin reminders** [M] ⚠ — calm, per-member, "took it" tap that
    just clears for the day — no streak, no adherence score. _(reuse: routines-style
    cards + recurrence.)_
16. **Prescription refill watch** [S] — "Léa's puffer refills in ~1 week." A date
    nudge tied to the pharmacy in Le cercle. _(reuse: cercle-businesses + reminder.)_
17. **Checkup cadence** [S] — dentist every 6 mo, optometrist yearly, kid's
    well-visit: "due for a checkup" derived from the last visit date, one tap to
    book-reminder. _(reuse: derived-date pattern.)_
18. **Sick-day log** [S] — a light note when a kid's home sick (fever, when it
    started) — useful for the doctor call and the "didn't we just do this" memory.
    Not a health tracker; a household memory. _(reuse: notes + member.)_
19. **Private cycle awareness** [M] ⚠ — opt-in, private-to-one-member period
    tracking that only ever surfaces as a gentle personal heads-up. Strictly calm,
    strictly private; never on the shared glance.
20. **Gentle wind-down / bedtime hour** [S] — after a set hour the kiosk dims to the
    bedtime routine + tomorrow's one big thing, then the screensaver. A household
    "the day is closing" cue. _(reuse: idle/ambient + audience.)_

## D · Pets (the other family members)

21. **Pet profiles** [S] — pets as cards in Le cercle: vet (already a business),

    cercle members + businesses; pet as a member-kind.)_
22. **Walk / feed / litter rotation** [S] — "who walks the dog" beside the kid chore

23. **Vet & vaccine due** [S] — rabies booster, flea/tick season, grooming —

## E · Getting around (transport & the morning shape)

24. **Vehicle care** [M] — oil/km, tire swap, SAAQ registration & licence renewal,
    insurance — a "vehicle member" with its own upkeep cadence and document vault.
    _(reuse: home-upkeep pattern + document vault.)_
25. **Morning briefing (spoken)** [M] ✦ — at wake, the kiosk reads the day aloud:
    weather, who has what, supper tonight, the one thing not to forget. The board's
    data, given a voice. _(reuse: `useSpeak` + board today data + audience.)_
26. **Departure mode / "leaving the house"** [S] — a one-screen pre-flight: lunches,
    backpacks, keys, "is the garage closed," today's weather coat call. _(reuse:
    `todos` departure templates — already an approved concept.)_
27. **Door-side mirror mode** [M] ✦ — a kiosk by the entrance shows the leaving-the-
    house essentials big: time, transit/commute, weather, the departure checklist.
    A surface preset, not a new app. _(reuse: surface presets + ambient.)_
28. **Carpool & ride coordination** [M] — who's driving which kid where, this week;
    the recurring soccer-carpool that lives in five text threads. _(reuse: events +
    cercle people as drivers.)_
29. **Transit / commute peek** [M] — next bus/train or drive-time for the saved
    morning trips, shown only in the morning window. _(reuse: postal + a transit API;
    degrade gracefully when unset, like AI/R2.)_

## F · Connection, coordination & the household's social life

30. **Who's home / status board** [S] ✦ — each face taps "out / back by ~6 / away
    for the weekend." A calm presence glance — answers "is anyone going to be home?"
    without a group text. _(reuse: MemberSwitcher + the return-home drift idea.)_
31. **Quiet handoff notes** [S] — co-parent → co-parent context that isn't a chore
    or event ("she barely napped," "field trip $ due Fri"). A typed/voice note
    addressed to a face. _(reuse: notes + member addressing.)_
32. **Tap-a-face poll** [S] — "tacos or pasta tonight?" / "movie pick" — everyone
    taps a face under an option. A calm decision, no leaderboard. _(reuse: members +
    a tiny vote, shows faces not counts.)_
33. **Gift & wishlist per member** [S] — each person's running wishlist + a private
    "who's getting what" so two parents don't double-buy. _(reuse: cercle gift-notes,
    already a concept; R2 for a screenshot.)_
34. **Babysitter handoff sheet** [M] — extends **guest mode** into a real handoff:
    today's plan, bedtime routine, allergies, the medical card, emergency contacts,
    wifi, house rules — all read-only, time-boxed. \*(reuse: guest mode + medical card
    - house map.)\*
35. **Guest welcome card** [S] ✦ — a QR by the door → wifi password, the bin day,
    "shoes off please," where the bathroom is. The house, explained to a visitor.
    _(reuse: guest token + house map + a public-ish read view.)_
36. **Grandparents' window** [M] ✦ — a read-only pane you share with extended family:
    the grandkids' calendar highlights, latest photos, birthdays. Cross-household
    warmth without giving away the keys. _(reuse: guest/read tokens + R2 frame.)_

38. **School admin hub** [M] — picture day, pizza day, permission slips, PED days,

## I · Memory, time & the long view (what only a years-long app can do)

46. **Family timeline** [M] ✦ — scroll your household's history: events, photos,
    milestones, meals-you-loved, on one ribbon. The app's quiet superpower is that it
    _remembers_. _(reuse: events history + R2 + milestones + "on this day.")_
47. **Year in review** [M] — a gentle annual recap: places, firsts, the most-cooked
    supper, photos — generated, not gamified. _(reuse: recap engine, scaled to a year.)_
48. **Trip planner & vacation mode** [M] — a countdown, a packing checklist, the
    itinerary, _and_ a "we're away" toggle that pauses chores/routines and the bin
    reminder so the kiosk doesn't nag an empty house. _(reuse: `todos` + a household
    away-state + recurrence suppression.)_
49. **Traditions tracker** [S] ✦ — "we always pick apples in late September,"
    "Sunday pancakes," the réveillon menu — recurring _traditions_, not tasks, that
    resurface their photos and notes from last time. _(reuse: yearly recurrence +
    timeline + R2.)_
50. **Time capsule / letters forward** [M] ✦ — record a voice or written note
    addressed to a future date ("open on her 10th birthday," "us, next New Year").
    The household talking to its future self. _(reuse: R2 audio memos + a future
    reveal-date, like derived birthdays.)_

### The three big unlocks (where these cohere into something bigger than a list)

- **Make the home / vehicle / pet first-class members.** Upkeep cadence, document
  vault, and "due soon" all fall out of one pattern: _an entity in Le cercle with a
  recurrence and some R2 docs._ Ideas 1, 2, 21, 24 are one feature wearing four hats.
- **"Due soon" as a calm, derived layer.** Bills, expiries, checkups, vaccines,
  registrations (8, 10, 16, 17, 23, 24) are all the **derived-date** pattern already
  proven by birthdays — _no event rows, just a quiet chip when something nears._ This
  is the single highest-leverage primitive on the list.
- **The household has a memory.** Milestones → timeline → year-in-review →
  traditions → time capsule (37, 46, 47, 49, 50) turn an always-on app's longevity
  into the thing no notebook or group chat can match. This is the emotional moat.
